Frequently asked questions

How does adoption work?

Browse pets, save your favourites, then submit an application. In a real adoption, the shelter reviews your details, arranges a meeting and completes the paperwork with you.

Are adoption fees charged?

This is a demonstration site, so no fees are charged. Real shelters usually ask for a modest fee that helps cover vaccinations, neutering and care.

Can I adopt if I live in a flat?

Often, yes. Many cats, small pets and calm dogs are happy in flats. Each profile lists special requirements so you can choose with confidence.

Can I meet a pet before I decide?

A meeting is a key part of any good adoption. Contact the shelter listed on the profile to arrange a visit.

What if it doesn’t work out?

Responsible shelters help with training advice first, and will take an animal back rather than see them re-homed carelessly.

Is this website a real shelter?

No. PawHaven is a frontend demonstration built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The pets, people and contact details are illustrative, and forms are not sent anywhere.
